# Trademark policy: the name „Based“

The code of Based is open source under AGPL-3.0-or-later ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE)). The license gives you rights to the
**code**. It gives you no rights to the **name „Based“, the Based logo or the domain basednotes.app**. These identify
the official project by Elija Samuel Friedrich-Ulrich, so people know which app and which cloud they are trusting with
their data.

## Allowed without asking

- Saying that your software is „based on Based“, „a fork of Based“ or „compatible with Based“, as long as it is clear
  that it is not the official project.
- Writing about Based, reviewing it, teaching it, linking to it.
- Using the name and logo unchanged to point to the official project (articles, talks, comparison tables).
- Building, running and self-hosting the unmodified code for yourself or your organisation under the name Based.

## Not allowed without written permission

- Publishing a fork, app, extension or hosted service **under the name „Based“** or a confusingly similar name
  (e.g. „Based Pro“, „BasedNotes“, „Based Cloud“ for your own service).
- Using the Based logo or the stencil „b“ for a modified version.
- Offering a hosted service that looks like the official Based Cloud.
- Domains, app-store listings or social accounts that suggest they are official.

## Forks

Forks are welcome. Give your fork its own name and logo, and remove the Based logo and the basednotes.app links from
it. You may keep a note like „Based on Based by Elija Samuel Friedrich-Ulrich“.

## Questions and permission

Write to **hello@basednotes.app**. If unsure, ask first.
