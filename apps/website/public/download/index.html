Download Based Beta for Mac

macOS 13 or later · free during the beta · updates itself

Install

  • Open Based-Beta.dmg and drag Based Beta into Applications.
  • Open Based Beta from Applications. If macOS asks before the first start, right-click the app, choose Open and confirm.
  • No account needed. Import from Notion on the welcome screen, or start empty.

Your data

Everything stays on your Mac. For automatic backups, turn on Settings → Sync & server → Local server. Export anything important now and then from the page menu or Settings → Import & export.

Feedback

Use Help → Report a Problem… (⇧⌘B) and attach the saved report to an email to hello@basednotes.app, or just write to us. The most useful feedback: what you expected, what happened instead, and whether it happens again.
