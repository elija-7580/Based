Where Based sits

Easy like Notion. Private like Obsidian.

Notion is easy, but your data lives in someone else's cloud. Obsidian keeps it on your machine, but asks you to become a plugin engineer. Based gives you both: set up in a minute, your data stays yours, and everything is there to tweak if you want to.

your data, your devices their cloud easy technical

Your data

You always know where your data is.

In the beta, everything lives on your Mac. There is no account and no server of ours in between. At release you choose where it syncs: Based Cloud on EU servers with end-to-end encryption, or a server of your own.

Now, in the Mac beta

On your Mac.

  • Pages and databases stored locally, offline by default
  • Every version of every page, backups to a folder on your Mac
  • Sync between your Macs through iCloud Drive (preview)
  • Export to Markdown with real file names, or a lossless archive
  • Open source under the AGPL, so you can read every line

At release

Our cloud or your server.

  • Based Cloud on EU servers, end-to-end encrypted
  • Self-hosting on a server of your own
  • iPhone, iPad and web, then Windows, Linux and Android

After the beta

Later, if you want them: agents.

Once the notes app is solid, opt-in agents come next. One main agent plans, hands work to specialists and checks back with you. They work inside your pages, mark every line they write and use the AI you already pay for. Based stays a complete workspace without them, and they stay off until you switch them on.
