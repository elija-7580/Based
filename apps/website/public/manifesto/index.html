Manifesto

We are rebuilding knowledge work.
Starting with what Notion got right.

Notion showed the world that notes, docs and databases belong together. We think they also belong on your own devices: open, fast, encrypted, and ready for agents that work for you without working behind your back.

Principles

01

You own your data. Full stop.

Your workspace lives on your devices and opens instantly, even on a plane. Nothing gets deleted behind your back. Based Cloud runs in the EU and keeps your devices in sync without being able to read a word. Your data survives any company, including ours.

02

Safety is not a pricing tier.

Version history, offline access and backups are basic rights for your own writing. Nobody should pay extra to undo a mistake from last month.

03

Agents sign their work.

AI is welcome here, on your terms. Off by default, on a leash you choose, and every line it writes is marked with where it came from.

04

Your AI, your keys.

You already pay for a model. Based connects to it instead of selling you a second one, and never hides which model did what.

05

Built in Europe, in the open.

Made in Vienna. MIT licensed, clean-room from public documentation, with the roadmap, the backlog and every night shift log in public.
