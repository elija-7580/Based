Proactive

It comes to you.

You don't check on agents. They check in: when something is done, when they need a decision, and every morning with a briefing. On your Mac, on your phone, in your inbox.

Native to your databases

Your morning briefing is a database row.

Agents don't live in a chat window next to your work. They read and write your databases like you do. Every briefing, run and decision becomes a row you can filter, relate and search, so the history of your work builds itself.

Any AI via MCP

Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and others read and write your workspace, with your rules.

Cloud agents are opt-in and only read the pages you hand them, while they work. Based Cloud runs on EU servers and is end-to-end encrypted by release. The beta starts on the Mac with local storage and iCloud.

Control

You set the leash.

Four positions, per task. Change it any time, even mid-run.

Default

Work freely, check in before changing your pages.

Agents research, draft and organise in their own space. Before they touch your pages, they ask, once per change set.

Night shift

Go to bed. Wake up to progress.

Schedule agents to run while you sleep: file the day's voice notes, refresh the project pages, prepare tomorrow's brief. In the morning, the marker shows exactly what changed.

True story: Based itself is built this way. A night shift of agents picks tasks from the backlog, runs the tests and leaves a log by morning.
