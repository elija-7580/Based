Where Based sits

Easy like Notion. Private like Obsidian.

Notion is easy, but your data lives in someone else's cloud. Obsidian keeps it on your machine, but asks you to become a plugin engineer. Based gives you both: set up in a minute, your data stays yours, and everything is there to tweak if you want to.
