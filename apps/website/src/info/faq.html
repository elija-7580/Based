Questions about Based

Short answers for people who want to try the Mac beta. Something missing? Write to hello@basednotes.app.

What is Based?

Based is a workspace for pages and databases that keeps your data on your Mac. It works like the Notion you know: pages inside pages, the / menu, tables, boards and calendars. The difference is where your data lives.

Is the beta free?

Yes. The beta is free.

How is Based funded?

By donations. Based has no investors and sells no ads. You can sponsor Based on GitHub with $3 a month or any amount, monthly or once. It is a donation: every feature stays free for everyone, and sponsors get nothing extra. The goal is 1,000 monthly sponsors. At 1,000, Based Cloud goes live for everyone.

Where is my data stored?

On your Mac. You don't need an account, and the beta does not send your pages anywhere. You can turn on automatic backups to a folder on your Mac and export everything as Markdown or HTML at any time.

Can I bring my Notion workspace?

Yes. Connect Notion directly or pick a Notion export (.zip). Based rebuilds pages, databases, relations and nesting locally, with normal file names. Big workspaces take a few minutes, and you can keep working while the import runs. Markdown, HTML, CSV, Obsidian, Evernote, Word and OPML imports are there too.

What can I do in the beta?

  • Pages and subpages with the block editor: text, lists, toggles, callouts, code, equations, images and files
  • Databases with table, board, list, gallery, calendar and timeline views, filters, sorts, groups, formulas, relations and rollups
  • Quick Find (⌘K), tabs, favorites, Trash and page history
  • Meeting notes, transcribed on your Mac. The speech model downloads once, then the audio never leaves your Mac.
  • Export a page or the whole workspace

What is not in the beta yet?

AI and agents, the graph view and real-time editing across devices. The beta is Mac only, without accounts, sharing or the Based cloud. iPhone, iPad, web, Windows, Linux and Android come later.

Which Macs does it run on?

macOS 13 or later.

How do I join?

Join the waitlist on the home page. We invite people from the list in small groups and send a download link by email.

Is Based open source?

Yes, under the GNU AGPL v3 (AGPL-3.0). The code is on GitHub.

How do I report a problem?

In the app: Help → Report a Problem… or ⇧⌘B. Reports contain no page text unless you tick “Include real text”. You can also write to hello@basednotes.app.
