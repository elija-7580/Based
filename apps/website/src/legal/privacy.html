Privacy policy

In short: no cookies, no advertising, no fonts or scripts from other companies. Visit statistics only if you allow them, counted on our own server. If you join the waitlist, we store your email (and your first name and use case, if you give them) on our own server in Germany, only to tell you when the beta opens. Write to hello@basednotes.app and we delete it.

Who is responsible

Elija Friedrich-Ulrich, Gföhl, Austria. Email: hello@basednotes.app. More in the imprint.

What this website does not do

  • No cookies, no advertising, no analytics from other companies. Our own visit statistics run only if you allow them (see below).
  • No fonts, scripts or images from other servers. The Schibsted Grotesk font is served from our own server.
  • The personal reply that appears after you join the waitlist is written by your browser itself. Nothing is sent to an AI or any other service for it.

Hosting

The website runs on a server of Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestraße 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany, in a data centre in Germany. We have a data processing agreement with Hetzner under Art. 28 GDPR.

To deliver a page to you, the server has to know your IP address. Our web server writes no access logs. To protect the forms from abuse, the waitlist service keeps IP addresses in memory for at most one hour to slow down too many requests from the same address. They are not stored. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Visit statistics (only with your consent)

If you click “Allow” in the privacy notice, our own Matomo installation counts your visit. Matomo runs on our server at Hetzner in Germany; nothing is sent to other companies. It processes the pages you visit, the website you came from, browser and device information, and an approximate location derived from a shortened IP address. Matomo sets no cookies, and if your browser sends “Do Not Track”, nothing is counted. Without your consent the Matomo script is not loaded at all.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time with “Privacy settings” at the bottom of the page. Your choice is stored only in your browser (local storage).

Beta waitlist

If you sign up for the beta, we store:

  • your email address,
  • your first name, if you give it,
  • what you want to use Based Notes for (the choice above the form),
  • the time you signed up.

We use this data only to tell you when the beta starts and to invite you. We do not share or sell it. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time: a short email to hello@basednotes.app is enough, and every email from us has an unsubscribe link.

The data is kept in a database on our server at Hetzner in Germany. We delete it as soon as you withdraw your consent, and at the latest 24 months after you signed up.

If we invite you, we also store your personal download link with its expiry date, how many times it was used (at most 5) and when it was last used (so an interrupted download can resume), so that only invited people can download the beta. No IP address is stored with it. We delete it 30 days after the link expires.

Ideas on the roadmap

If you submit an idea on the roadmap, we store only the text, the area you chose and the time, without a name or email address. Please do not put personal data in the field. Votes for individual items are counted only in your browser and are not sent to us.

Problem reports from the app

In the Based Notes beta app you can report a problem with ⇧⌘B and “Send to Based”. Only when you click it, the app sends one report to our server at basednotes.app. A report contains:

  • the sentence you write about the problem,
  • the app version, the system (for example macOS), the window size, the theme and the app's recent log, with email addresses and access keys removed,
  • your last steps in the app (keys, clicks, changes) and the structure of the open page. The text of your pages is replaced with scrambled letters, unless you choose to include the real text,
  • a screenshot of the app window, if “Include screenshot” is switched on. A screenshot shows what is on your screen, so you can switch it off before you send.

No account, name, email address or IP address is stored with a report, and reports are not linked to the waitlist. We use them only to find and fix bugs. The legal basis is your consent when you send the report (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

The report is stored on our server at Hetzner in Germany and deleted there after 90 days. To work on it, our server copies it into our project tracker in Notion. Notion is run by Notion Labs, Inc., San Francisco, USA, which processes the reports for us as a processor (Art. 28 GDPR). Notion may process data in the USA, based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. We do not send waitlist addresses to Notion. The app shows a report ID after sending: write it to hello@basednotes.app and we delete your report on the server and in Notion.
