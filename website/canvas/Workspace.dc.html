Backups

Three copies. Zero panic.

Data loss is the thread that never leaves r/Notion. Notion's own export can't recreate a workspace, and paid backup services say a complete restore isn't possible through Notion's API. With Based you own your data: it lives in three places, nothing gets deleted behind your back, and restoring is a click.

Sync and cloud

Your phone gets everything. The server gets gibberish.

The beta syncs your Apple devices through iCloud. At release, Based Cloud takes over: servers in the EU, and everything encrypted on your device before it leaves, so the server only ever stores scrambled bytes. Prefer your own hardware? Run the server yourself.

  • EU servers, end-to-end encrypted by release. iCloud in the beta.
  • Cloud agents are opt-in. They only see pages you hand them, while they work.
  • Self-host with one command, same apps.
  • Offline is the default. Every row, every formula. Sync catches up later.
